## Cart and Checkout Information

- To add a product to your cart, click the **Add to cart** button on any product card or product detail page.
- To remove a product, click **Remove** on the product card, detail page, or inside the cart.
- The cart badge on the header icon shows the current number of items (maximum 6).
- Cart contents are preserved as you navigate between pages.
- To begin checkout, click **Checkout** from the cart page.
- Checkout Step 1 requires: First Name, Last Name, and Zip/Postal Code. All fields are mandatory.
- Checkout Step 2 displays a full order summary including item total, tax, and order total.
- After clicking **Finish**, you will see a confirmation: "Thank you for your order!"
- Completing checkout clears the cart automatically.
